What we build

Four parts of one integrated system.

The project

Port, storage and processing. One complex, one operator.

We close the gap between port and processing. Soybeans arrive by sea, move by conveyor into our silos and feed the plant directly. Finished products leave through the same berth or by rail.

01 · PORT

Dedicated deep-water berth

Handymax to Panamax vessels of 50 to 60 kt. Unloading at about 1,500 t per hour, a 60 kt vessel in 2 to 2.5 days. Throughput up to 7.5 Mt per year, including commercial grain.

02 · STORAGE

Silo park with 11 to 12 weeks of autonomy

450 kt of steel silos in Phase 1, expandable to 900 kt. Direct feed to the plant, oil tanks and meal warehouses on site.

03 · PROCESSING

Extraction plant, 5,000 t per day

1.5 to 1.7 Mt per year in Phase 1, 3.4 Mt at full scale. Meal, oil and lecithin on CPM Europa Crown technology.

Why soy, why now

The world grows soy in the Americas and consumes it everywhere else.

01

Structural demand

Three markets at once: feed protein, food ingredients and industrial use including biofuels. Soy is less exposed to any single demand factor.

02

Geographic gap

Production sits in the United States, Brazil and Argentina. Consumption sits in Europe, the Middle East, North Africa and Central Asia. Logistics and processing are where value is created.

03

Regional protein deficit

The EU, MENA and Central Asia are net importers of protein. A processing hub next to these markets lowers the cost of feed.

04

A bet on the chain, not the price

Control of port, storage and processing close to demand, instead of a bet on the price of beans.

Poti FIZ · 42 ha · Black Sea
Why Poti

The shortest sea route between the Americas and the markets we serve.

Poti is Georgia's largest seaport and the Black Sea gateway of the Middle Corridor. From here, Southern Europe, the Eastern Mediterranean, the Middle East, North Africa, the Caucasus and Central Asia are all within reach.

0% VAT and dutiesFree Industrial Zone regime on imported feedstock and exported products. Zone operating since 2008.
42 ha, long-term leaseIndustrial site with expansion reserve, owned by the Government of Georgia.
Own berthNot part of the commercial port. One system with the plant, no intermediaries on the export leg.
Multimodal accessSea, rail and road. Meal to Central Asia by rail, oil by sea, lecithin in containers.
