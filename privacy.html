Privacy policy

Effective 1 October 2026. This policy explains how Golden Grains Group ("we") processes personal data submitted through goldengrains.org.

What we collect

When you request the investment teaser we collect your name, company, work email and the type of organisation you represent. We also collect standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited.

Why we use it

  • To send you the requested materials and reply to your enquiry.
  • To verify that requests come from professional investors, lenders and partners.
  • To keep the website secure and understand how it is used.

Legal basis and retention

We process data on the basis of your request and our legitimate interest in communicating with prospective partners. Enquiry data is kept for 24 months after the last contact, unless a longer period is required by law.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share it with advisers bound by confidentiality, such as financial and legal advisers involved in the project, and with service providers that host this website and email.

Your rights

You may ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its processing, by writing to info@goldengrains.org.

Cookies

This website uses only technically necessary cookies and a remembered language preference. No advertising cookies are set.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page.
